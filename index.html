The Process

How FindIt Works

A simple four-step process to reunite campus community members with their lost belongings.

1
📷

Snap a Photo

Found something? Take a clear photo of the item right where you spotted it on campus to document it.

2
📝

Fill the Details

Describe the item, choose its category, and add any details that would help the owner identify it's theirs.

3
📍

Pin the Location

Mark the exact spot on the campus map where the item can be collected, so the owner knows where to go.

4
🤝

Get Reunited

The owner sees the listing, verifies ownership, and collects their item at the pinned location.

💡 Tips for a Good Listing

  • 📸Take clear, well-lit photos from multiple angles
  • 🔍Mention unique identifiers — color, brand, engravings
  • 📍Be specific about the exact collection point location
  • ⏰Post as soon as possible for faster reunions
  • 🔒Never publicly expose personal data from ID cards

📞 Need Help?

For urgent items like university IDs or wallets, contact campus security directly.

About FindIt

Built for students,
by students 💚

FindIt is a campus-wide lost and found platform that makes it effortless to report, discover, and reclaim lost items. Whether you're a student, professor, or staff — everyone's part of our finder community.

We believe small acts of honesty build a stronger campus culture. By using FindIt, you're not just recovering a phone or keys — you're helping build trust across the university.

  • 💚
    Community first — every campus member can contribute and benefit
  • 🔒
    Privacy safe — we never expose sensitive personal information publicly
  • ⚡
    Fast & simple — post a found item in under 2 minutes, no account needed
  • 📍
    Location pinning — clear pickup points so there's never any confusion
